# Privacy

[Español](PRIVACY.es.md) · **English**

YerbaLauncher **does not collect, send or share any data**.

- **No internet permission:** the app cannot connect to any server.
- **No ads, analytics or trackers.** No third-party libraries.
- **What it stores, only on the device:** your settings, the position of your icons and the list of
  hidden apps. A factory reset (Settings) or uninstalling the app deletes everything.
- **Permissions:** `ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE` / `ACCESS_WIFI_STATE` only to show the IP address in the
  optional system line; `REQUEST_DELETE_PACKAGES` only to open the system uninstall dialog when you ask;
  `<queries>` (launcher intent only) to list your apps in the drawer.
- **Nothing runs in the background:** the launcher only works while you are looking at it.

Questions: open an [issue](https://github.com/matcarfer/YerbaLauncher/issues).
